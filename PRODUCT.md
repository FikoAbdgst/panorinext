# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People celebrating personal moments (birthdays, graduations, reunions, casual gatherings) and people running professional or community events (brands, offices, weddings). The landing page must speak to both without narrowing the product to one occasion.

## Product Purpose

PanoriCam is an online photobooth. Visitors choose a frame, take photos in the browser, and leave with a digital photostrip or GIF. The landing page should make the product immediately understandable, desirable, and easy to start.

## Positioning

A browser-based photobooth built around custom, playful frames and immediate digital output. No installation and no physical booth are required.

## Operating Context

Used on phones and desktops at events and in casual settings. Users browse frames, select a free or paid frame, take a photo session, then download PNG or GIF results and optionally send them by email. Event organizers can request custom frames through WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frame catalog with categories, slots, free and paid frames.
- In-browser photo capture, PNG photostrip export, and GIF creation.
- Email delivery for photo and GIF results.
- QRIS and bank-transfer payment flows for paid frames.
- Custom frame requests by WhatsApp.
- Admin tools exist for frames, categories, transactions, and testimonials.
- Do not present unverified customer counts, ratings, or commercial claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PanoriCam.
- Keep the existing cream, brand-red, and light-red palette with Poppins.
- Voice is playful, warm, and Indonesian-first.
- The logo and existing product imagery remain part of the identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Frame data and categories from Supabase, including free and paid frames.
- Testimonials stored in Supabase; use only real entries.
- Existing public imagery in `public/images/`, logo in `public/logo.png`, and QRIS asset in `public/qris.jpg`.
- No verified "1000+ customers" figure; do not reuse that claim.

## Product Principles

- Make the first action obvious and fast.
- Let the frames and photo strips carry the personality.
- Feel playful without obscuring how the product works.
- Show real product output rather than generic photobooth decoration.
- Keep both personal and professional occasions welcome.
